Drag & drop your podcast

MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, MP3, M4A, WAV. No size limit.

Clip settings

Reels and TikTok are tall, feed posts square, YouTube wide. Changeable per clip afterwards.

The best moments, spread across the episode.

How carefully the speech is transcribed — the bulk of the wait. Fast is roughly 3× quicker than Balanced with slightly rougher wording; Most accurate is about 2.5× slower. Try Fast on a long episode.

Clips are cut from your original footage, so 4K stays sharp. Shrinking first re-encodes the whole episode and makes clips softer — only worth it if you are short on disk space.

Captions

Word-by-word, the style Reels and TikTok use. Off gives you clean footage — you get a subtitle file either way.

Choose a file above to get started.

How it works

  1. 01 Reads the file and shrinks it if it's oversized
  2. 02 Transcribes every word, with timings
  3. 03 Scores the episode for clip-worthy moments
  4. 04 Crops to the speaker and burns in captions

What comes out

  • An MP4 per clip, cut to shape
  • A poster frame for each one
  • An .srt beside every clip
  • The full episode transcript
